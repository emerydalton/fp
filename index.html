Start here

  • Why Functional Programming Matters John Hughes. The short paper that explains the point of it all.
  • Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs Abelson & Sussman. The classic foundation, in Scheme.
  • How to Design Programs Felleisen et al. Systematic program design, in Racket.
  • OCaml Programming: Correct + Efficient + Beautiful Cornell CS 3110. A modern free textbook with videos.
  • Haskell MOOC University of Helsinki. Free course with auto-graded exercises.
  • Learn You a Haskell The friendly illustrated introduction, community edition.
  • F# for Fun and Profit Scott Wlaschin. Explanations that carry over to any language.
  • Mostly Adequate Guide to Functional Programming Professor Frisby. FP in JavaScript.
  • Exercism Free practice tracks for Haskell, OCaml, Elixir, Clojure, F#, and more.
