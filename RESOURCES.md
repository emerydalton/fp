# Functional programming resources

A curated, opinionated list. Free unless marked 💲.

## Start here

- [Why Functional Programming Matters](https://www.cs.kent.ac.uk/people/staff/dat/miranda/whyfp90.pdf) — John Hughes. Higher-order functions and laziness as tools for modularity. Read it first, then again in six months.
- [Functional Programming Jargon](https://github.com/hemanth/functional-programming-jargon) — plain-language glossary with examples.
- [Exercism](https://exercism.org/tracks) — free mentored practice tracks for Haskell, OCaml, Elixir, Clojure, F#, Scala, Elm, and more.

## Foundations (language-agnostic thinking)

- [Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs](https://sarabander.github.io/sicp/) — Abelson & Sussman. Scheme. The classic.
- [How to Design Programs](https://htdp.org/) — Felleisen et al. Racket. Systematic program design; the gentlest serious start.
- [Programming Languages, Part A](https://www.coursera.org/learn/programming-languages) — Dan Grossman, University of Washington. ML, then Racket and Ruby in parts B and C. Free to audit.
- [Grokking Simplicity](https://www.manning.com/books/grokking-simplicity) 💲 — Eric Normand. FP for working programmers: actions, calculations, data.

## By language

### Haskell

- [Haskell MOOC](https://haskell.mooc.fi/) — University of Helsinki. Free course with auto-graded exercises.
- [Learn You a Haskell (community edition)](https://learnyouahaskell.github.io/) — the friendly illustrated introduction, kept up to date.
- [CIS 194 (Spring 2013)](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~cis1940/spring13/) — Brent Yorgey's UPenn course; the homework is the value.
- [Learn Haskell by building a blog generator](https://learn-haskell.blog/) — project-based.
- [Typeclassopedia](https://wiki.haskell.org/Typeclassopedia) — the map of Functor, Applicative, Monad, and friends.
- [Programming in Haskell](https://www.cs.nott.ac.uk/~pszgmh/pih.html) 💲 — Graham Hutton. Concise and rigorous.

### OCaml

- [OCaml Programming: Correct + Efficient + Beautiful](https://cs3110.github.io/textbook/) — Cornell CS 3110 textbook, with a companion video series.
- [Real World OCaml](https://dev.realworldocaml.org/) — practical, industrial OCaml.

### F#

- [F# for Fun and Profit](https://fsharpforfunandprofit.com/) — Scott Wlaschin. Excellent explanations that carry over to any language.
- [Domain Modeling Made Functional](https://pragprog.com/titles/swdddf/domain-modeling-made-functional/) 💲 — Scott Wlaschin. Types as a design tool.

### Lisp family

- [Clojure for the Brave and True](https://www.braveclojure.com/) — Daniel Higginbotham.
- SICP and HtDP, above.

### JavaScript / TypeScript

- [Professor Frisby's Mostly Adequate Guide to Functional Programming](https://github.com/MostlyAdequate/mostly-adequate-guide)
- [Functional-Light JavaScript](https://github.com/getify/Functional-Light-JS) — Kyle Simpson. Pragmatic, no category theory.

### Others

- [An Introduction to Elm](https://guide.elm-lang.org/) — small, pure language; a quick way to feel FP in a UI.
- [Functional Programming in Lean](https://lean-lang.org/functional_programming_in_lean/) — FP with dependent types.
- [Functional Programming in Scala](https://www.manning.com/books/functional-programming-in-scala-second-edition) 💲 — "the red book". Demanding exercises.

## Papers

- [Why Functional Programming Matters](https://www.cs.kent.ac.uk/people/staff/dat/miranda/whyfp90.pdf) — Hughes, 1989.
- [Out of the Tar Pit](https://curtclifton.net/papers/MoseleyMarks06a.pdf) — Moseley & Marks, 2006. State as the root of complexity.
- [Monads for functional programming](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/wadler/papers/marktoberdorf/baastad.pdf) — Wadler, 1995.
- [Purely Functional Data Structures](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~rwh/students/okasaki.pdf) — Okasaki's thesis, 1996.

## Talks

- [Simple Made Easy](https://www.infoq.com/presentations/Simple-Made-Easy/) — Rich Hickey.
- [Functional Design Patterns](https://fsharpforfunandprofit.com/fppatterns/) — Scott Wlaschin.
- [Railway Oriented Programming](https://fsharpforfunandprofit.com/rop/) — Scott Wlaschin. Error handling by composition.

## Going deeper

- [Category Theory for Programmers](https://github.com/hmemcpy/milewski-ctfp-pdf) — Bartosz Milewski.
- [Software Foundations](https://softwarefoundations.cis.upenn.edu/) — Pierce et al. Proofs and programs in Coq.
- [Types and Programming Languages](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~bcpierce/tapl/) 💲 — Benjamin Pierce.
